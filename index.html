Enterprise systems, finance & governance transformation

  • Architected and led implementation of the Group's first integrated enterprise operating environment, the Unique Group Integrated Enterprise Ecosystem (UG-IEE), unifying finance, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, HR, payroll, projects, customer management, asset management, and executive reporting across eight subsidiaries in manufacturing, hospitality, real estate, construction, trading, and corporate services.

Supply Chain & Digital Transformation Lead

June 2019 – February 2024

Led the redesign of the organisation's agricultural supply chain into a fully integrated digital operating environment, connecting approximately 30,000 contracted farmers, regional field operations, procurement, logistics, manufacturing and factory processing, finance, inventory, compliance, and export functions into a single traceable ecosystem.

  • Designed and led implementation of a national farm-to-export traceability platform, establishing 100% product traceability from farmer registration through procurement, transport, processing, export, and final customer verification.
  • Introduced a digital farmer management framework capturing farm locations, production records, certification status, quality history, and supplier information in a single trusted system, strengthening farmer profiling and compliance with organic and Fairtrade certification requirements.
  • Overhauled procurement operations, replacing manual buying with structured digital workflows for farmer verification, quality assessment, pricing, transaction approval, and documentation, and integrated digital weighing technology at the point of purchase to improve transaction accuracy and settlement confidence.
  • Eliminated pricing and quality fraud previously introduced by field officers, securing the company's actual farm-gate price, lifting raw-material quality, and strengthening farmer loyalty and trust in the buying process.
  • Cut raw-material weight loss from roughly 30% under the previous ad hoc collection model to under 2% once collections moved to a weekly schedule, and to zero once collections moved to daily pickup from field stores straight to factory dispatch.
  • Designed an automated payment process linking approved procurement transactions directly to mobile money disbursement, accelerating farmer payments, reducing cash-handling risk, and strengthening supplier trust.
  • Established end-to-end inventory visibility across procurement, collection centres, transport, warehousing, and factory processing, and extended traceability into processing batches and export consignments to support quality assurance and export compliance.
  • Developed management reporting and operational dashboards giving leadership timely visibility into procurement, farmer activity, inventory, logistics, production, and payments, enabling faster operational and strategic decision-making.
  • Supported delivery of internationally funded agricultural development programmes while maintaining compliance with Fairtrade, Organic, Ecocert, KEPHIS, KEBS, OSHA, NEMA, and Agriculture and Food Authority requirements.
  • Led cross-functional stakeholder coordination across field officers, agronomists, factory teams, finance, logistics, quality assurance, exporters, technology partners, and executive leadership to embed the transformation across the organisation and sustain adoption.

Transformation outcome. Converted a manually managed agricultural supply chain into a connected digital ecosystem that eliminated pricing and quality fraud, secured accurate farm-gate pricing, lifted farmer loyalty and trust, and achieved full traceability and compliance with local and international standards. Standardised, data-backed processes and SOPs lifted performance consistently across field, factory and support teams, giving leadership trusted, evidence-based information across the value chain.
